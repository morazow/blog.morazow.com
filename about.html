I am a Senior Software Engineer at Ververica, the original creators of Apache Flink. I contribute to the Apache Flink, Flink CDC and Apache Fluss open-source projects, and currently I am a member of the Stream Storage Team that leads the enterprise Fluss product development.

Before Ververica, I was at Exasol, where I designed and maintained open-source integrations between the Exasol database and the data ecosystem: connectors for Apache Kafka, Apache Spark and cloud storage. Before that, I built real-time streaming and analytics pipelines at GfK.

I hold dual master's degrees in distributed systems from KTH Royal Institute of Technology (KTH) and Instituto Superior Técnico (IST).

I also give conference talks about streaming and data engineering topics.
